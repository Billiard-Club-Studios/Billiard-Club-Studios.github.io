Piki Kiki privacy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Publisher

Billiard Club Studios (Kevin Weiss) E-mail: billiardclubstudios@pm.me Address: see the Impressum. Contact: see Support. Piki Kiki has no account and no login. The studio runs no server for the game and does not receive your saved game.

Data on your device

Piki Kiki stores on your iPhone or iPad your level progress, your best score, your gem balance and gem history, difficulty settings the game adjusts on your device while you play, how many retries have passed since the last ad and when it was shown (to space ads), how many attempts you made on each level, your sound setting, your analytics choice, and a random installation ID used for iCloud sync. Google's consent software also stores your consent choices on your device. The studio does not receive this data. It stays until you delete the app and can be part of a backup of your device made with Apple's tools.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Piki Kiki syncs your level, best score and gem and booster counters between your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage (not CloudKit). The random installation ID is part of the stored key name. Nothing else is synced: not your difficulty data or ad counters. The data is in your iCloud account, the studio cannot read it, and Apple provides the service under its own privacy policy. Your highest level and best score are kept, and the gem counters of your devices are added up. You manage iCloud in Settings under your name, iCloud. The data stays in iCloud until you remove it there. Deleting the app does not delete it from iCloud, and a reinstall restores your progress from it.

Gameplay statistics

Piki Kiki can send gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase. They contain: a launch counter and whether it is your first session; the level, attempt number, score, target score, moves allowed and used, and gems earned, when a level starts, is won or is lost; gem earnings and spending with amounts and balance; and ad events (whether an ad was offered, shown or failed, the ad network and the result). A coarse label chosen by the game from how you play (easy, standard or hard) and a version number of the difficulty rules are sent with each level start. They contain no name, e-mail or free text. Google's analytics software also records a few standard events on its own, such as the first opening of the app, sessions, engagement time and app or operating system updates.

Google gives your installation a random app-instance ID and receives technical data such as device model, operating system, app version and language, and derives your approximate location from the IP address. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. The statistics are tied to this random ID, not to your name.

Statistics are switched on only when Google's consent check allows it. Where consent is required, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, they stay off unless you agree to the storage of information on your device; until the game knows where you are, it treats consent as required. Elsewhere they are on until you switch them off. Advertising-related signals are enabled only if you also allow tracking when iOS asks. You can switch statistics off at any time: Pause, Settings & Privacy, Share Usage Stats. The switch shows whether statistics are really being collected. If your privacy choices do not allow it, nothing is sent even if the switch is on. Legal basis: where consent is required, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes the data for us under the Google Analytics terms. Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. User-data retention resets with new activity of the same installation, so it runs from the last activity. Event-data retention does not reset. These settings do not affect most standard aggregated reports.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Piki Kiki shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob. An ad can appear when you retry a level after losing: at most on every second retry, at least 90 seconds after the last ad, never on levels 1 and 2, never in your first session, and not after three losses in a row on the same level. Ads are limited to Google's General content rating. The game includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. No ad is requested before Google's consent check has finished. Google's SDKs declare that they process, for advertising and analytics, device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), advertising data, how you interact with ads, approximate location, and performance, crash and diagnostic data. Google also receives technical data such as your IP address with each ad request. The game does not pass your progress, score or gems to Google. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and tracking permission

When you tap Play, Google's consent SDK checks whether consent is required. Where it is, Google's consent form is shown before the first level. If it cannot be shown or loaded first, for example because you are offline, the game tries again later and shows it at a break, such as the lose panel after you lose a level. You can consent, decline or manage options. Where the form is required, you can change your choice later in Pause, Settings & Privacy, Advertising Preferences. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised. Google can still serve limited ads, which switch off personalisation and features that need a local identifier, so ads can still appear. After you complete your first level, if ads are allowed, iOS may ask whether Piki Kiki may track you across other companies' apps and websites. iOS asks at most once. If you refuse, the number of ads stays the same, but they are less tailored and use no advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Legal basis: where you consent, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). For limited ads without consent, our legitimate interest in financing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links and support

The Privacy Policy and Support buttons open the studio's web pages in an in-app browser; those pages have their own policy. The game sends no e-mail itself: the Support button can copy the studio's e-mail address to your clipboard so you can write from your own mail app. The game has no purchases, no location access and does not collect your contacts.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Piki Kiki has no crash-reporting service of its own and sends no diagnostics to the studio. Google's ad SDK collects performance and crash data as described above.

Contact

If you write to billiardclubstudios@pm.me, we process your e-mail address, your message and the data you send, only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our e-mail service is Proton Mail (Proton AG, Switzerland). We keep correspondence for 24 months after the matter is resolved and then delete it, unless statutory retention duties or legal claims require longer. This applies only to e-mails.
