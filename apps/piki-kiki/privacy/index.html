Piki Kiki privacy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Publisher

Billiard Club Studios (Kevin Weiss) E-mail: billiardclubstudios@pm.me Address: see the Impressum. Contact: see Support. Piki Kiki has no account and no login. The studio runs no server for the game and does not receive your saved game.

Data on your device

Piki Kiki stores on your iPhone your level progress, your best score, your gem balance and gem history, difficulty settings the game adjusts on your device while you play, how many retries have passed since the last ad and when it was shown (to space ads), how many attempts you made on each level, your sound setting, your analytics choice, and a random installation ID used for iCloud sync. The studio does not receive this data. It stays until you delete the app and can be part of a backup of your iPhone made with Apple's tools.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Piki Kiki syncs your level, best score and gem and booster counters between your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The random installation ID is part of the stored key name. Nothing else is synced: not your difficulty data or ad counters. The data is in your iCloud account, the studio cannot read it, and Apple provides the service under its own privacy policy. Your highest level and best score are kept, and the gem counters of your devices are added up. You manage iCloud in iPhone Settings under your name, iCloud. The data stays in iCloud until you remove it there. Deleting the app does not delete it from iCloud, and a reinstall restores your progress from it.

Gameplay statistics

Piki Kiki can send gameplay statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase. They contain: a launch counter; the level, attempt number, score, target score, moves used, and gems earned, when a level starts, is won or is lost; gem earnings with amounts and balance; and ad events (whether an ad was offered, shown or failed, the ad network and the result). A coarse label chosen by the game from how you play (easy, standard or hard) is sent with each level start. They contain no name, e-mail or free text.

Google gives your installation a random app-instance ID and receives technical data such as device model, operating system, app version and language, and derives your approximate location from the IP address. Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. The statistics are tied to this random ID, not to your name.

Statistics are switched on only when Google's consent check allows it. Where consent is required, for example in the EEA and the UK, they stay off unless you agree to the storage of information on your device. Elsewhere they are on until you switch them off. Advertising-related signals are enabled only if you also allow tracking when iOS asks. You can switch statistics off at any time: Pause, Privacy & Ads, Share gameplay statistics. Legal basis: where consent is required, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes the data for us under the Google Analytics terms and keeps event-level statistics for 2 months. Data tied to the app-instance ID is kept for up to 14 months, and the 14 months start again whenever the same installation is active again.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Piki Kiki shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob. An ad can appear when you retry a level after losing: at most on every second retry, at least 90 seconds after the last ad, never on levels 1 and 2, never in your first session, and not after three losses in a row on the same level. Ads are limited to Google's General content rating. You can report an ad in Pause, Privacy & Ads, Report an ad; that opens an e-mail to the studio. The game includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. No ad is requested before Google's consent check has finished. Google's SDKs declare that they process, for advertising and analytics, device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), advertising data, how you interact with ads, approximate location, and performance, crash and diagnostic data. Google also receives technical data such as your IP address with each ad request. The game does not pass your progress, score or gems to Google. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and tracking permission

When you tap Play, Google's consent SDK checks whether consent is required. Where it is, Google's consent form is shown before the first level; if it cannot load first, it appears at the lose panel when you lose a level. You can consent, decline or manage options. Where the form is required, you can change your choice later in Pause, Privacy & Ads, Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, ads are not personalised. Google can still serve limited ads, which switch off personalisation and features that need a local identifier, so ads can still appear. After you complete your first level, if ads are allowed, iOS may ask whether Piki Kiki may track you across other companies' apps and websites. iOS asks at most once. If you refuse, the number of ads stays the same, but they are less tailored and use no advertising identifier. You can change this in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. Legal basis: where you consent, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). For limited ads without consent, our legitimate interest in financing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Crash reports and diagnostics

Piki Kiki has no crash-reporting service of its own and sends no diagnostics to the studio. Google's ad SDK collects performance and crash data as described above.

Contact

If you write to billiardclubstudios@pm.me, we process your e-mail address, your message and the data you send, only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Our e-mail service is Proton Mail (Proton AG, Switzerland). We keep correspondence for 24 months after the matter is resolved and then delete it, unless statutory retention duties or legal claims require longer. This applies only to e-mails.

Retention and deletion

  • On your device: until you deletethe app.
  • iCloud: until you remove it fromyour iCloud storage.
  • Statistics at Google: event-leveldata 2 months; data tied to theapp-instance ID up to 14 months,restarting when the sameinstallation is active again. Wecannot normally find your data inAnalytics because it is tied to arandom ID, not to you.
  • Ad data at Google: set by Google,not by the studio.
  • Consent choices: on your deviceuntil they expire or you changethem.

To delete your data, delete the app, remove Piki Kiki data in your iCloud storage settings, and use Google's privacy tools for data held by Google.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in the USA and other countries. Google states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses standard contractual clauses where required.
