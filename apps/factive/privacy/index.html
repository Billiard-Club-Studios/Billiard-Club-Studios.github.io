Factive: 10k+ Facts privacy

This policy covers the iPhone app Factive: 10k+ Facts. Factive has no account and no login.

Who is responsible (controller)

Kevin Weiss, Billiard Club Studios E-mail: billiardclubstudios@pm.me Address: see the Impressum.

On your device and in iCloud

Factive saves your topics, saved facts, read facts and some settings on your iPhone. The studio does not receive this data. It stays until you delete the app and may be in your iPhone backup.

If you use iCloud, Factive syncs topics, saved facts and read facts between your devices through Apple's iCloud storage. The studio cannot read this data; Apple provides the service under its own privacy policy. Deleting the app does not delete it from iCloud.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Factive shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob, about every 15 facts. Google states that its advertising software can process device identifiers (including the advertising ID, if you allow tracking), ad data, approximate location and diagnostic data, and receives your IP address with each ad request. Factive does not pass your facts or reading history to Google for ads. Google handles it under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and tracking

When you open Factive, Google's consent check decides whether a consent form is needed, for example in the EEA and the UK. You can agree, decline or manage options, and change your choice later in Settings > Advertising preferences (shown only where Google requires it). No ad is requested before the check has finished. Without consent, Google states that ads are not personalised, but it may still show limited ads.

If ads are allowed and you did not decline personalisation, iOS may then ask whether Factive may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you refuse, Google states that ads are shown without the advertising ID. You can change this in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Legal basis: your consent where given (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). For limited ads without consent, our legitimate interest in financing a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Usage statistics

Factive can send usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase: for example how many facts are viewed, which topics are on, how often facts are saved and when an ad is shown, plus device model, operating system and language. Google gives your installation a random ID and derives your approximate location from the IP address; Google states that Google Analytics does not log or store IP addresses. The statistics are tied to this random ID, not to your name, and contain no fact text, name or e-mail. If linked to AdMob, Google may add ad impression and revenue data.

Statistics are sent only when Google's consent check allows it. Where consent is required, they stay off unless you agree; elsewhere they are on until you switch them off in Settings > Share usage statistics. Without consent, turning the switch on opens Google's privacy-options form where Google offers it.

Legal basis: where consent is required, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes the data for us under the Google Analytics terms.

Other

Factive sends no diagnostics to the studio. The link button on a fact opens the source website in your browser; it has its own policy. For hosting, see Privacy.

How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you deletethe app.
  • iCloud: until you remove it fromyour iCloud storage.
  • Consent choices: on your deviceuntil they expire or change.
  • Usage statistics at Google (ourpolicy): event-level data 2months; data tied to the random IDup to 14 months, restarting whenthe installation is active again.
  • Ad data at Google: set by Google.
  • E-mails tobilliardclubstudios@pm.me: 24months after the matter isresolved, then deleted, unless thelaw requires longer.

To delete your data: delete the app, remove Factive data in your iCloud storage settings, use Google's privacy tools, or write to billiardclubstudios@pm.me.

International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in the USA and other countries. Google states that it complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses standard contractual clauses where required.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 15 to 18, 20, 21 and 7(3) GDPR). Because statistics are tied to a random ID, we can normally not find your data in them. Write to billiardclubstudios@pm.me. We make no automated decisions.
